## Analysis

The development feedback shows consistent `ReferenceError: eventually is not defined` in `lib/operations.ts:79`. This occurs in the `closeEditor` function where `eventually` is used but not imported.

Looking at the editable files:
- `tests/lib/operations.ts` uses `eventually` at line 79 but does not import it
- `tests/lib/adapt.ts` exports `eventually` 
- `tests/lib/operations.ts` imports from `adapt.ts` but does not include `eventually` in its import statement

The fix is to add `eventually` to the import statement in `operations.ts`.

No other issues are indicated by the feedback - all failures are due to this single import omission.
